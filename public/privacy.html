Privacy notice

Last updated 20 October 2026

Who we are

SG QuickFix is operated by [COMPANY NAME], UEN [UEN]. This notice explains what personal data we collect, why, and what you can ask us to do with it under Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA).

Contact our Data Protection Officer at [CONTACT EMAIL].

When you get an estimate

Your category, description and the options you choose are processed in your browser to calculate the estimate. We don't store them, and you don't need an account.

When you use the quote assistant

Your messages, the job details on the page and your photo, if you added one, are sent to our AI provider, Anthropic, so the assistant can reply. Anthropic processes this data under its commercial terms. We don't keep a record of your chat. Our hosting provider, Netlify, keeps short-lived technical logs, including IP addresses, to run the service, limit abuse and fix errors.

Please don't share your NRIC number, full address, phone number or payment details in the chat. The assistant doesn't need them.

When you apply to list your business

We collect the details in the application form: business name, UEN, your name, WhatsApp number, email, website, services, licence numbers, areas, prices, description and logo. Netlify stores these submissions for us.

We use them to check your business and licences, to contact you about your application, and to publish your listing. A published listing shows your business name, description, areas, starting price, licence number, contact number, website and logo. It never shows your email address or UEN.

Usage counts

To see how many people use the site, we count page views, estimates, chats, clicks on booking links and supplier applications. Each count stores the page, the kind of action (for example the repair category, or which booking link you clicked) and a code made by scrambling your IP address and browser details together with the date. The code changes every day, so it can't be used to follow you over time, and we never store your IP address. Nothing is saved in your browser. If your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal, we don't count you at all.

Other sites

Links to Carousell, booking platforms, maps, government registers and partner websites take you to services we don't control. Their own privacy policies apply. When you send a job summary on WhatsApp, the text goes through WhatsApp only when you choose to send it.

Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when you visit. We don't set cookies and we don't use advertising trackers.

Transfers outside Singapore

Netlify and Anthropic may process data on servers outside Singapore. We use providers that are bound by contract to protect it to a standard comparable to the PDPA.

How long we keep it

Supplier applications and listings are kept while your listing is active, and deleted within 12 months after it ends or when you ask us to remove them, unless we need to keep them longer by law. Usage counts contain no personal details and are kept for up to two years.

Your choices

You can ask to see or correct the personal data we hold about you, or withdraw your consent for us to use it. Email [CONTACT EMAIL] and we'll respond within 30 days.
